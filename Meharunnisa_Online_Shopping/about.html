ABOUT OUR WEBSITE

Shopping made simple, elegant and enjoyable.

Meharunnisa Online Shopping is an e-commerce website developed as a B.Tech academic project.

Our website provides customers with an easy way to explore fashion, accessories, footwear and jewellery products in one place.

The website is designed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a clean, modern and responsive interface.

Technologies Used

  • HTML5 – Website Structure
  • CSS3 – Website Design
  • JavaScript – Website Interaction

Our Vision

To make online shopping simple, elegant and enjoyable by bringing fashion, accessories, footwear and jewellery together in one beautiful shopping experience.
